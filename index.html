Grandpa Runs Sidewalk Splat

Grandpa is on the loose, and what he drops from above is not birdseed. Run, jump and dodge through the suburbs, a beach boardwalk, a shopping mall, busy city streets and up a skyscraper in this cartoon side-scroller for iPhone and iPad.

  • Five hand-drawn levels, each with its own music
  • Simple touch controls: move and jump
  • Earn Bronze, Silver and Gold medals and Poop Shards for beating target times
  • Spend shards on costumes for you and for Grandpa (they only change how you look)

Need help? See Support or email twocrazymojo@yahoo.com.
